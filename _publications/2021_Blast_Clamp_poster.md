# Abstract
A low-cost wire clamp for Ilizarov Ring Fixators was designed and optimized. The preliminary design was achieved through morphological analysis. Design for manufacturability was specifically considered during this design selection with the intention of cost reduction. Ansys Workbench was used to perform static structural analysis on the proposed clamp. Several geometric parameters were identified and optimized using response surface optimization method focusing on ‘avoiding wire slippage in the clamp’, ‘reduction of clamp mass’, and ‘ensuring no-failure under the applied forces’. The FEA analysis showed that the final design can avoid wire slippage while maintaining a mass of 125.73 g without failure.
