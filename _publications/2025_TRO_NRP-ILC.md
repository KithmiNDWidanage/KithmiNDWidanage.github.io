excerpt: 'This paper is about a novel iterative learning controller (ILC) for learning time-invariant properties of an unknown environment irrespective of the path it takes during different iterations. The proposed method has been demonstrated for learning reference path, orientation, and environment stiffness while performing force tracking. Compared to traditional ILC methods, this method does not need the robotic process, path, or exploration regions to be repeated in each iteration.'
